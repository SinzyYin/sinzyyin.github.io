## Key Measures Explained

### 1. Index Value
Shows how strongly an attitude is associated with the audience.
- 110+ means strong likelihood  
- 90–110 means normal range  
- <90 means weak  

Example:  
An index of 187 means this group is *very strongly* likely to behave in that way.

### 2. Vertical Percentage  
Shows **what percentage of the group** actually holds that attitude.  
Values above 50% were bolded since they represent majority behaviour.

Using both together ensures the attitudes chosen are **statistically strong** and **meaningful in real-world targeting**.
