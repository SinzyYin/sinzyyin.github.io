Completed in November 2025 with Toshiba Malaysia, this project involved collecting data through Google Forms and presenting the findings in a Canva research report. These insights then guided the development of a creative brief and two supporting storyboards.

Through this project, I learned to:

- **Translate complex data into clear visual reports** using people charts, rankings, and word clouds
- **Optimize colour choices and labels** for readability
- **Turn research insights into strategy** through a creative brief
- **Use AI-powered image generation tools**, such as ChatGPT, to produce two storyboards


### Toshiba Research Report
The data collection wasn’t anything fancy, as my focus was on the **visualization** aspect, that is, how to translate raw responses into something more digestible, memorable, and easy to understand.

I enjoyed experimenting with *people charts*, *rankings*, and *word clouds*. Since this was an assignment with a strict maximum of five pages, I had to sacrifice some depth and clarity to meet the requirement.

### Creative Brief

The creative brief follows the **[JWT (2008) Creative Brief Template](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BLQBXB3O0RKWnUDp4HkiJZmwCwhcPLQz/view)**. It’s just a solid, reliable option, and I chose it because it’s clear, practical, and works well not only for this project but potentially for future ones too. I might come across better creative brief templates later on, but I’ll see how it goes.
